=========== 2. LibVLC initialises (the single change the macOS build most depends on) ===========
Program.Main calls LibVLCSharp.Shared.Core.Initialize() at Program.cs:43, AFTER
'[SingleInstance] TryAcquire: listening' and BEFORE Avalonia starts. Both of those lines are
above, so Initialize() returned instead of throwing VLCException as it did before
VideoLAN.LibVLC.Mac was added. The positive form of the same fact - the native libraries
actually mapped into the running process:
<repo>/src/AgentEyes.DesktopApp/bin/Release/net8.0/LibVLCSharp.dll
<repo>/src/AgentEyes.DesktopApp/bin/Release/net8.0/libvlc/osx-arm64/lib/libvlc.dylib
<repo>/src/AgentEyes.DesktopApp/bin/Release/net8.0/libvlc/osx-arm64/lib/libvlccore.dylib
