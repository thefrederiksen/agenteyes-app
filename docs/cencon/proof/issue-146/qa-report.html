Findings that are not #146 defects

  • F1 - the default gate launches 276 ffmpeg processes (above).
  • F2 - a race between two processes: one host's repair pass picks up a recording that another host on the same library is still capturing. It admits it for resume with "no claim" and burns a mux attempt on the half-written raw.mp4 (C1 take 8, installed app pid 16160). Here it failed safely, on an ffprobe read, at the cost of 1 of the 3 attempts. The RecordingWorkset claim is per-process, and the capture does not hold mux.lock. It only matters when two hosts share a library, for example the tray app plus agenteyes serve. Worth an issue.
  • F3 (cosmetic) - MicChainSampleRateTests.ExecutedGraph's stall message always says "44.1 kHz source", even for the 22.05 kHz case.
