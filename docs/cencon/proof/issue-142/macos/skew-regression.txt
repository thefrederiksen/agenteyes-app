=== IS THAT RESIDUAL SLOPE DISTINGUISHABLE FROM ZERO? ===
  slope        = 52.02 ppm
  std error    = 39.29 ppm
  t            = 1.32 on 16 degrees of freedom
  95% interval = -31.3 to 135.3 ppm
  residual scatter (1 sigma) = 35 ms

  |t| < 2.12, so the slope is NOT significant at 95%. The deficit is consistent with a
  CONSTANT ~259 ms stop latency, and any drift is too small to resolve in this data.

=== WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE 1.5 s BOUND ===
  Worst case inside the 95% interval: 135.3 ppm.
  Total shortfall(T) = 1.047 s + drift*T. At the 95%% upper bound that reaches 1.5 s at:
    T = 3348 s = 0.9 hours
  At the point estimate (52 ppm): T = 8707 s = 2.4 hours
